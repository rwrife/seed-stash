Seed Stash keeps your seed-packet inventory on your device. Record crop, variety, supplier, lot, packed date, storage location, and estimated seed count. Log dated sowing adjustments and measured germination samples; sample results do not predict packet viability. Flag packets for shopping and find low-stock packets.

Export your packet inventory as CSV or save a versioned JSON backup. Preview packet counts before confirming a restore that replaces the local inventory. No account, cloud sync, or internet connection is needed for inventory tracking.
